# Roadmap

## Phase 1: MVP foundation
- landing page and messaging refinement
- mockup editor shell with device frame concept
- export CTA and free/pro conversion flow
- basic product testing coverage

## Phase 2: Product-market validation
- more layouts and templates
- brand color presets
- save and restore mockup projects
- better onboarding and walkthrough

## Phase 3: Growth and monetization
- Stripe subscriptions
- team accounts and workspace collaboration
- asset library and premium templates
- analytics and conversion tracking

## Phase 4: Scale and enterprise
- agency tier
- white-label paths
- custom export settings
- advanced sharing and review workflows

## Recommended success criteria

- landing page conversion to signup
- completion of first mockup export
- conversion from free to Pro
- repeat product usage over 30 days
