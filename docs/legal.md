# Legal starter guide

This document is a practical starter for legal preparation, not legal advice.

## Recommended baseline

- Use a standard open-source license for the public codebase if you intend to open-source the repo (MIT is a strong default for a developer tooling SaaS project).
- Add a terms of service for the hosted service and product usage.
- Add a privacy notice explaining data collection, analytics, and storage.
- Create a security disclosure policy for handling vulnerabilities.
- Check local regulations before collecting personal data or processing transactions.

## For this project

Recommended defaults:
- MIT license for codebase
- Terms of Service for app usage
- Privacy Policy for account and analytics data
- Security Policy for bug reporting
- Cookie and analytics notice when cookies are used

## Product-specific legal notes

Because this is a SaaS mockup tool, important legal concerns include:
- ownership of user-generated content
- export rights and commercial use
- licensing of templates and asset packs
- data retention and deletion
- subscription billing compliance

## Recommended next step

Before launching paid usage, consult a local attorney or reliable SaaS legal service to review:
- commercial terms
- privacy laws
- billing practices
- user-content ownership
- business entity setup
