# AppMockupCreator Launch Plan

## Core launch objective

Convert interested users into early adopters and then into paying customers by proving value quickly and clearly.

## Launch sequence

### 1. Preparation
- Final landing page
- Product screenshots and demo mockups
- Signup flow
- Billing setup
- Product onboarding
- Example exports

### 2. Beta launch
- Offer early access to a limited audience
- Ask for feedback on speed, output quality, and value
- Optimize onboarding based on user experience

### 3. Public launch
- Publish to Product Hunt
- Share on social channels
- Post demo videos and sample visuals
- Collect testimonials
- Keep the CTA simple: Start free

### 4. Conversion optimization
- Add pricing page
- Improve onboarding based on drop-off points
- Segment users by product use case
- Offer referrals or waitlist rewards

### 5. Growth loop
- Publish educational content around app launch visuals
- Show real mockup examples from users
- Encourage sharing on social platforms
- Use early wins to build trust

## Metrics to watch
- Landing page conversion
- Waitlist signups
- Trial starts
- Export completion rate
- Free to paid conversion
- Retention after 30 days
