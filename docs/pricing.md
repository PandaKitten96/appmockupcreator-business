# AppMockupCreator Pricing Plan

## Recommended pricing model

### Free
- 3 projects
- 5 exports per month
- Basic mockup templates
- Watermarked exports
- Best for trying the product

### Pro — $19/month
- Unlimited projects
- Premium templates
- High-resolution exports
- Commercial use rights
- Custom branding features
- No watermark
- Best for founders and creators

### Team — $49/month
- 5 seats
- Shared workspaces
- Collaboration features
- Team template library
- Priority support
- Best for product teams and agencies

### Agency / Enterprise — custom pricing
- Custom seat counts
- White-label options
- Branding workflows
- Dedicated onboarding
- Best for clients and larger design teams

## Monetization logic

The pricing should be intentionally simple and tied to usage value:
- Free: enough to get adoption
- Pro: strong daily-use value
- Team: collaborative value and account expansion
- Enterprise: bigger client work and custom workflows

## Recommended conversion path

1. Create a polished free experience
2. Deliver useful exports early
3. Offer premium assets and quality upgrades
4. Convert creators to Pro
5. Upsell teams to Team and Agency plans

## Pricing principles

- Keep the free tier useful, not empty
- Make Pro clearly worth upgrading
- Keep pricing understandable
- Focus on output value rather than complexity
